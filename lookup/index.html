Free identity screen

Already have the USDOT number? This checks whether the carrier shares contact identifiers with other registrations under FMCSA's own published chameleon-carrier threshold. It returns a flag and counts only; it never names another carrier.

About these results

  • The lookup reads FMCSA's public records. Each answer states the date its records are current as of. It is not live.
  • FMCSA stopped updating its legacy licensing archive on 2026-05-14. When a docket status comes from that archive, the result says so, and the status describes that date. Confirm current authority with FMCSA before relying on it.
  • A flag from the identity screen is a data pattern, not a finding of fraud. In a hand review of flagged clusters, a third were legitimate affiliated fleets. Use it to decide what to look at next.
  • This page makes the same free calls as the API, limited to 60 requests a minute per IP address. This page sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The service's own operational logs are covered by the Privacy Policy.
