A key comes with $5 of credit, enough for about 50 carrier selection records or 500 carrier lookups. You can run checks in your browser on the Run a check page, set the key once in your MCP client, or send it as a header. A call is charged only when it returns records. We read each request by hand, usually within one business day, and email you the key and how to use it.

What we do with this

We use your answers to decide whether to issue a key, to email you the key, and to ask how it is working. We do not sell them or add you to a mailing list. The Privacy Policy has the details.
