5. Freshness and accuracy

FMCSA builds its data snapshots from a database up to 24 hours old and states that the data does not reflect real-time information. We refresh the Service from FMCSA's releases about once a month. Every response states the date its records are current as of (record_as_of), and you must evaluate each answer as of that date. A carrier's authority, insurance or out-of-service status may have changed since then. Before relying on an answer for a decision that matters, confirm current status with FMCSA directly.

Some fields are our own derivations, for example safety_tier_derived, identity match scores, cluster assignments and flags. They are computed signals with known false-positive rates, described in the responses themselves. They are not facts about a carrier and not findings of fraud or wrongdoing. A "flagged" or "linked" result is a reason to look further, never a conclusion. The Service may contain errors, omissions or normalization artifacts, inherited from the source data or introduced by our processing.
