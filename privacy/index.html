Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what 4SAKE Agent Co ("we") collects when you or your agent use FMCSA Carrier Intelligence (the "Service") and this website, and what we do with it. We have no user accounts, so we collect very little.

What we collect when you call the Service

  • Network information. Your IP address, the time of each request, and standard request metadata such as the user agent. We use the IP address for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Request details. Which tool you called and the names of the arguments you sent. For paid calls whose payment verified, we also record the argument values, such as the USDOT numbers you looked up.
  • Payment details. For paid calls: your paying wallet address, the amount, the network and the blockchain transaction signature. Payments settle on the public Solana blockchain, so the transaction is publicly visible on the blockchain independently of us.
  • Outcome. Whether the call returned records, was free, or failed, and how long it took.

Before a payment verifies, we record only argument names, never values. We do not ask for your name, email address or any account information, and we do not use cookies on the Service.

Why we use it

  • To operate the Service, enforce rate limits and prevent abuse.
  • To keep an accurate, tamper-evident billing record and reconcile it against the blockchain.
  • To investigate billing disputes and refund requests.
  • To measure reliability and performance.

We do not sell this information, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you. We may disclose it if the law requires us to, or to protect the Service and its users.

Where it is kept and for how long

The Service runs on Google Cloud in the United States. Google acts as our infrastructure provider. Operational logs are kept for about 30 days. Billing records (the payment and request details above) are kept for up to seven years, for accounting and dispute purposes. We then delete them or keep them only in aggregate form.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to operate and secure its service, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Carrier data

The carrier information the Service provides comes from FMCSA's public records about regulated motor carriers as businesses. The Service does not return carriers' names, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses. Requests to correct a carrier's underlying record should go to FMCSA, which is the source of the data. Questions about how the Service presents it can go to the address below.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about a wallet address or IP address you control, or to ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it, email us from an address we can verify against your request.
