Pricing

Last updated: October 17, 2026. Prices in US dollars per call.

You pay only when a call returns records. A carrier that does not exist, a search with zero results, or a service error costs nothing. There is no subscription and no minimum.

How you pay

API key with prepaid credit

The simplest route for a team without a crypto wallet. Your agent or script sends the key on each call, or you can run checks in your browser. Each charged call is deducted from the credit. Request a free evaluation key: it comes with $5 of credit, about 50 carrier selection records or 500 carrier lookups, and each request is reviewed by hand, usually within one business day. It is for evaluation. Card and invoice payments are not set up yet; if you need one, say so in the request.

x402, USDC on Solana, Base or Polygon

No account and no key. A paid call without credentials gets an HTTP 402 stating the price, and an x402-capable client pays it from a USDC wallet and retries. Details are on For agents.

What the price does and does not buy

  • Records built from FMCSA's public data, each response dated with the day its records are current as of.
  • For about 72% of Active-authority carriers, authority and insurance-on-file fields describe 2026-05-14, because FMCSA stopped updating its legacy archives on that date. Responses say so.
  • A record, not a recommendation or a score. Nothing here says a carrier is safe or unsafe, and it is not legal advice or a consumer report.
